Pulzivo pricing

Pulzivo meters events. A page view, a click and a custom event each count, so the same traffic can use more events here than pageviews on a tool that only counts pages. Run the free plan for a few weeks before you pick a paid tier. These are the plans on the pricing page.

  • Free is $0: 1 website, 10,000 events/month, 30-day retention. Page views, clicks, custom events, scroll depth and Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP and CLS).
  • Starter is $9/month: 2 websites, 100,000 events/month, 6-month retention. Adds sessions, unique visitors and UTM attribution.
  • Pro is $29/month: 5 websites, 500,000 events/month, 12-month retention. Adds opt-in user identification, performance metrics and JavaScript error capture.
  • Enterprise is $99/month: unlimited websites and events, 24-month retention. Adds form tracking and abandonment, rage clicks and dead clicks.

Founder offer: 50% off any paid plan for 12 months with code FOUNDER50, limited to the first 10 paying customers. Enter the code at checkout.

Goals and revenue are recorded with trackGoal() and trackRevenue(). A weekly email, on by default, summarizes visitors, pages, sources and what changed. Settings can create a read-only share link, and can import a GA4 CSV without connecting a Google account. Improve your site turns the events your plan collects into a short list of fixes.
