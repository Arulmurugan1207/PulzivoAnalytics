What UTM parameters are

A UTM parameter is a name on the query string of a link you control. Analytics tools, including Pulzivo on the Starter plan, read those names and group visits by campaign. Without them, an email, an ad, and a social post that land on the same page collapse into one referrer, or into direct traffic when the app strips the referrer.

  • utm_source is where the click came from: google, newsletter, x.
  • utm_medium is the kind of channel: cpc, email, social, video.
  • utm_campaign is the campaign name: spring-launch.
  • utm_term is the paid keyword, when you have one.
  • utm_content is which creative or link position: hero, footer.

Best practices

  • Use lowercase and hyphens. google and Google become two rows if you mix them. This builder lowercases the five values for you.
  • Keep one spelling per source. Pick newsletter and stay with it.
  • Put tags on links you control: ads, email, social, partner posts. Do not tag links inside your own site. An internal tag overwrites the campaign that landed the visitor.
  • Source, medium, and campaign are the three that matter. Leave term and content empty when you have nothing honest to put there.
  • The tagged URL is only a link. It does not track anyone by itself. Measurement starts when the destination page runs an analytics script.
